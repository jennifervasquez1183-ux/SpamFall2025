Jennifer Vasquez

Editor | Producer | Documentarian | Hunter College

B.A. in Arts - Aspiring Professor of Art & Production. My work explores transformation, movement, and labor through intimate portraits of women in NYC. Butterfly Gallery documents diverse labor — care work, salon work, dance — examining how movement shapes identity.

"I teach how to see — film is not just what we watch, it's how we understand each other."

Cibao Baila
